# V2 Research Questions

The V2 architecture is designed to support questions beyond the original K=4 experiment.

## Surface representation

1. Do local texture and morphology descriptors reveal structure that is not visible in the original five-variable feature space?
2. Which derived features most strongly separate the discovered surface-property regimes?
3. Does the expanded representation reduce dependence on any single physical variable?

## Model discovery

4. Which regimes are stable across model families rather than being artifacts of one clustering algorithm?
5. How does model agreement vary across feature-space density and cluster boundaries?
6. Does the V1 K=4 solution remain stable when local-context features are introduced?

## Spatial organization

7. Which regimes form coherent large-scale objects versus fragmented patches?
8. Where do high-ambiguity boundaries occur?
9. Are statistical anomalies concentrated near known geological boundaries, or do some occur in otherwise ordinary-looking regions?

## Geological interpretation

10. Which independently mapped geological categories show enrichment or depletion relative to regional background?
11. Where does the surface-property model disagree with the mapped geological context?
12. Which candidate anomalies deserve targeted geological review because they combine high statistical anomaly, high model agreement, and weak known-unit association?

These questions are intentionally framed as empirical tests. The V2 system does not treat an ML anomaly as proof of a new geological unit.
